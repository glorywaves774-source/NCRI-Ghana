Take action with NCRI-Ghana

Be Part of the Change

Stronger communities begin with collective action. We believe every child deserves protection and education, every woman and girl deserves equal opportunities, every young person deserves a chance to thrive, and every community deserves the resources and support to build a resilient future.

Join us to advance child protection and education, women’s and girls’ empowerment, sustainable agriculture and rural livelihoods, youth development and employment, climate and community resilience, and water, sanitation and hygiene. Together, we can turn community priorities into practical, lasting change.
