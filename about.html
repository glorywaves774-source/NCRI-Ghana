How we work

Our Approach

We believe that effective development must be community-driven, inclusive, evidence-informed and accountable. Our work emphasises:

1

Community-led planning

Engaging communities and local leadership in identifying priorities and developing appropriate responses.

2

Partnerships

Collaborating with traditional authorities, schools, local institutions, civil society organisations, public agencies and development partners.

3

Capacity strengthening

Supporting individuals, community groups and local structures to develop the skills and knowledge needed to sustain progress.

4

Protection and inclusion

Promoting safe participation and ensuring that vulnerable groups are considered in programme planning and implementation.

5

Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL)

Using appropriate evidence, feedback and lessons learned to improve programme quality and assess results.

6

Sustainability

Encouraging community ownership and locally appropriate solutions that can continue beyond individual projects.

Global goals

Our Commitment to the Sustainable Development Goals

NCRI-Ghana’s programme priorities align with relevant United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 1: No Poverty, SDG 2: Zero Hunger, SDG 4: Quality Education, SDG 5: Gender Equality, SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation, SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth, SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities, SDG 13: Climate Action, SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions, and SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals.

Through our community-focused work, we seek to contribute to these global development priorities in ways that respond to local needs and circumstances. Our contribution is demonstrated through programme activities, community participation and verified results. This alignment does not imply formal endorsement or partnership by the United Nations.

Our promise

Our Commitment to Communities

NCRI-Ghana is committed to listening to communities, respecting local knowledge, protecting the rights and dignity of individuals, and supporting practical solutions to local development challenges. We recognise that lasting progress requires trust, collaboration, accountability and sustained participation.

Together with communities and partners, we aim to help create a future in which children are protected and educated, women and girls are empowered, young people participate meaningfully, families have improved opportunities, and communities are better equipped to respond to challenges and pursue sustainable development.
